PROPOSAL FOR:

**Mental Health application that keeps track of events, positive or negative, and reacts/adapts to the user and their needs**

The figma draft here isnt the full design at all, but rather a flow and skeleton of how the app will work. I plan to flesh this out in a full mockup/prototype, and apply this to the app (as well as have the agent review this).


## Goal
My goal is to create a mental health application that keeps track of events, positive or negative, and reacts/adapts to the user.

## Account and Questionnaire
The user will make an account and fill out an easy-to-answer questionnaire to start their experience and tailor the app to their needs. They will answer their most common stressors, their daily habits, and their personality.

## Mental Health Check-Ups
The app itself will focus on the user's mental health through check-ups. It can send reminders for the user to answer small and easy-to-answer questions. Most questions will take open-ended answers through text, but also have the option for a dropdown menu to pick a suitable answer for the user's comfort, depending on the question.

## Event Logger
Another key feature will be an event logger for positive or negative events. It will allow the user to record events and view them later on a calendar (color coded). This will be important in determining the user's needs and the next steps for what the app should do.

## Exercises
The app will also include short exercises such as breathing or calming exercises, and more. The user will be able to choose whether to start an exercise, rather than being forced into one; the most it'll do is recommend an exercise.

## Other Notes:
I still need to flesh out how much the app should be further personalized, such as the theme or the dashboards changing based on the user's needs (feeling positive or negative). 

For personalization, the app should have a hidden "score" or value(s) that dictate what the user may need. The app will have different states that will change based on this user, such as being in a state where the user has a problem or worry (such as feeling down/worried due to an issue at work, which is ongoing), or if the user is in a positive state. One explicit instance of personalization that I want to implement is the app taking less of a proactive/present approach and prescense when the user is in a much calmer state for a longer period of time. Positive affirmations will be utilized as well.

## Research Connection
- The STAI supports present-moment check-ins, but this app's shorter questionnaire will not be presented in such a diagnostic manner.
- The mouse-click study didn't validate click speed as an anxiety measure, so the app will rely on self-report rather than claiming to detect anxiety from clicking (unnessecary).
- The mobile DBT study supports accessible mood logging, reflection, and skills. It also shows the importance of keeping the app simple, relevant, and reliable.

## R/D Findings
- **R1, D2, D3:** Exercises will stay under 90 seconds, use one step at a time, and minimize required reading or typing.
- **R2, D4:** Crisis-support information will remain visible and will not depend on the questionnaire or model.
- **R3, R4:** Users can skip, stop, change direction, and choose whether to follow a recommendation.
- **D1, D5:** Personalization will use saved history while keeping the interface simple and user-controlled.
- **D6:** The interface will use calm visuals and neutral, non-judgmental language.
